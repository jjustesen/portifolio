JJ / OPTICAL LANGUAGEMICROINTERAÇÕES · ESTUDO 01

UM SISTEMA DE MOVIMENTO

Between light
and language.

Números, matéria e palavras.
Uma experiência de leitura em movimento.

01 / Arrival

Every interface begins
with a moment of attention.

Numbers move before words arrive.
A small interval between expectation
and understanding.

Move your pointer through the letters.
Let the light follow, then settle.

02 / Material

Light is never still.

A field of shadows breathes beneath
the surface. Grain gives it a texture.
Your movement leaves a quiet trace.

The page responds locally.
A disturbance passes through the words,
then gives them back their shape.

03 / Reading

Clarity has a place.

Here, in the middle of the screen,
the words become clear.
At the edges, they turn into memory.

Scroll slowly. Watch the letters stretch
into fine vertical trails and dissolve.
Return, and they find their form again.

04 / Continuity

Motion serves
the reading.

One shared language.
A restrained palette.
Space for the content to breathe.

Johannes Justesen
Frontend / Interaction / Systems
